# Archived 15-cell Woodland Hero art experiment

`Woodland_Hero.blend` and `build_hero.py` preserve the earlier 15-cell cliff composition with cedars, bridge, stream and waterfall. The Unity menu **Diceforge > Woodland > Build hero level** rebuilds its prefab, materials and lighting profile as an art study.

Campaign levels 01-06 now use the shared `FirstTrail8.prefab` and eight-cell layout. Rebuilding the Woodland Hero art does not reassign a campaign map. The earlier captures and validation notes are historical references, not the current level 05 configuration.
