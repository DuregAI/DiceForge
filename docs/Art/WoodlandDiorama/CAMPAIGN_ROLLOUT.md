# Woodland campaign visual rollout

The nine numbered campaign maps use the diorama presentation through their existing `BattleMapConfig`, `GameModePreset`, `BattleLauncher` and `BattleRunner` path. Levels 01-06 share the hand-composed eight-cell `FirstTrail8.prefab`; the old generic level 01 field and 15-cell Woodland Hero field are no longer assigned to these campaign maps. Levels 07-09 retain their larger provisional woodland compositions. Experimental maps and separate standard modes are unchanged.

Demo RC v0.1 gameplay is now authored in `Demo_Level_01..06`, the corresponding `RulesetPreset` and deterministic dice bags. Levels 01-06 use `SoloTrail` with 1/1/2/3/3/3 named friends on side A. L1 offers step 1; L2-4 offer 1 or 2 for one action; L5-6 spend 1 and 2 separately in either order. Bark blocks cell 4 on L4/L6. Ryzh starts on 3 on L5, advances after a completed turn, and retires permanently if a friend occupies his next cell. Side B occupancy represents these obstacles, without B turns, bearing off or victory. The existing result and reward transaction path remains in use.

The rebuilt red and blue goblins supply Tish and Luma. Bum and Ryzh currently use adapted goblin prefabs, with an ochre backpack/scarf and orange tuft respectively; Bark has a simple round wooden beetle proxy. These are gameplay placeholders, not the final character art from the design document. The demo HUD shows location, arrived friends, named selection buttons, available steps, remaining actions, obstacle guidance and pause/restart. The opponent score and reroll are hidden. OUT has a separate ring beyond cell 7. The old debug presentation is hidden while the diorama HUD is active.

To inspect: open `Assets/_Project/06_Scenes/MainMenu.unity`, enter Play Mode, and launch any numbered level through the normal map. The repeatable editor command is **Diceforge → Woodland → Enable playable dioramas for levels 01-09**. For balance changes alone, use **Diceforge → Woodland → Use eight-cell trail for levels 01-06**. The full builder needs a clean edit-mode scene and the FBX sources generated by `build_goblin_candidate.py`.

For current gameplay verification, see `docs/Validation/DemoRCStage2/README.md`. The earlier 2/2/2/3/3/4 two-sided campaign captures are historical references. The rollout/rebalance commands preserve authored demo counts and validate their definitions. **Diceforge → Demo RC → Configure levels 04-06** recreates the stage-two configuration and missing proxy assets without replacing existing art. FirstTrail8 still has no saved landscape/portrait lighting references; the existing lighting validation flags this. Final portrait/device playtesting, dialogue tutoring, story scenes and mid-level checkpoints remain separate work. Levels 07-09 retain their older provisional compositions and rules.
